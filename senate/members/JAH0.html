Recorded votes 2009 to 2014, 186th to 188th General Court. The record on this page begins where the readable record does, not necessarily where this member's service did.

The Senate's roll calls of 2009 to 2012 are read from the Journal of the Senate, the Senate's own daily record, because the legislature's data feed and the Clerk's sheets do not cover those years. Of the 410 roll calls the Senate numbered in 2009 and 2010 this site has 181 with every name; the online Journal prints a placeholder, and no names, for 173, and 56 fall on days whose Journal is not online. Of the 316 numbered in 2011 and 2012 it has 299; 2 print no names and 15 fall on days not online. A senator's page counts only the votes this site holds, so for 2009 and 2010 it is not their whole record, and a vote missing here is not an absence. The Journal online for these years is the Clerk's unproofed daily text; every number without a page is listed, with its reason, on the accuracy page.
