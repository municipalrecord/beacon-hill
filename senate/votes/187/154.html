Question on passing item 8910-0105, contained in section 2, the reduction of His Excellency the Governor to the contrary notwithstanding

 ·  Senate roll call No. 154  ·  2012-03-01 14:09  ·  187th General Court (2011 to 2012)
35 yeas, 0 nays  ·  as printed in the Journal of the Senate (PDF), page 5
The Clerk's sheet was not archived: the legislature's server has no Clerk's sheet for this roll call (asked 31 October 2026). The names here are the Journal's.

The Journal: “Question on passing item 8910-0105, contained in section 2, the reduction of His Excellency the Governor to the contrary notwithstanding” After remarks, the question on passing item 8910-0105, contained in section 2, the reduction of His Excellency the Governor to the contrary notwithstanding, was determined by a call of the yeas and nays, as required by Chapter I, Section I, Article II, of the Constitution, at nine minutes past two o’clock P.M., as follows, to wit (yeas 35 — nays 0) [Yeas and Nays No. 154]:. The yeas and nays having been completed at twelve minutes past two o’clock P.M., item 8910-0105, contained in section 2, stands, notwithstanding the reduction of His Excellency the Governor, two-thirds of the members present and voting having approved the same.
