Election of the President of the Senate

Senate roll call No. 1  ·  2011-01-05 12:00  ·  187th General Court (2011 to 2012)

Nobody answers yea or nay on a roll like this one, so the legislature's data feed returns it with no names and its sheet does not download. The names above are transcribed from the Journal. This roll call is left out of every member's counts.
