Establishing a special commission to study the implementation of vocational education annexes in gateway cities

Messrs. Cronin, Lewis, Payano, Collins and Pacheco moved that the proposed new text be amended in section 2, by inserting after section 106 the following section:- SECTION 106A. (a) There shall be a special commission to study the implementation of vocational education annexes in gateway municipalities as defined in section 3A of chapter 23A of the General Laws.
