Amendment 871, moved by Michael O. Moore  ·  Adopted

Blackstone Visitor Center in Worcester

Mr. Moore moved that the proposed new text be amended in section 2, in item 7008-0900, by adding the following words:- “; provided further, that not less than $50,000 shall be expended for the operation of the Blackstone Heritage Corridor Visitor Center in the city of Worcester”; and in said section 2, in said item 7008-0900, by striking out the figure “$848,176” and inserting in place thereof the following figure:- “$898,176”.
