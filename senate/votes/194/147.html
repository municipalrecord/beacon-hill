Amendment 99, moved by John F. Keenan  ·  Adopted

Manet at the Quincy Public Schools

Mr. Keenan moves that the proposed new text be amended in section 2A, in item 1596-2517, by inserting the following words:- “; provided further, that not less than $199,800 shall be expended to the Quincy Public Schools for a program with Manet Community Health Center that will involve both students and staff by offering services including, but not limited to, acute and routine primary care integrated with on-site behavioral health services".
