An Act authorizing the conveyance of certain parcels of real estate between the department of conservation and recreation and the town of Holden

SECTION 1. Notwithstanding chapter 30B of the General Laws or any other general or special law to the contrary, the town of Holden may convey the following 2 parcels of land to the commonwealth to be held under the care, custody and control of the department of conservation and recreation for water supply protection and passive recreation: (i) a parcel located off of North street, shown on town assessors’ map 44 as parcel 3, containing approximately 9.67 acres of land; and (ii) a parcel located off of Meadow Wood drive, shown on town assessors’ map 161 as parcel 3, containing approximately 11.65 acres of land.
