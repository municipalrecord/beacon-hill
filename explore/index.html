The data

Every recorded vote, every member, every bill and every committee vote the legislature has published, in tables you can search, sort and filter. Most rows link to a page here, and every page back to the legislature's own record. The 2011 to 2017 House sheets are scans, so those rows carry the Clerk's printed tally but no names, and are marked as such. Every table is also a CSV, and what is in each column says what the columns hold.
