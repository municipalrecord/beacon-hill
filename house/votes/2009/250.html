Printed tally: 152 yeas, 1 nay, 8 not voting  ·  the Clerk's sheet (PDF volume)

The Clerk printed 152 yeas, 1 nay and 8 not voting, and the House Journal, page 748, records the same 152 yeas and 1 nay. The marks on the sheet's grid of names add up to 151 yeas, 1 nay and 8 not voting. The printed tally adds up to 161, one more than the 160 names the sheet prints. At least one mark on the grid is wrong, and nothing on record shows which, so no names are shown here and the vote is left out of every member's record. See the methods page.
