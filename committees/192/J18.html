Joint Committee on Mental Health, Substance Use and Recovery

J18  ·  192nd General Court  ·  House chair: Adrian C. Madaro  ·  Senate chair: Julian Cyr

Joint Committee on Mental Health, Substance Use and Recovery It shall be the duty of the Committee on Mental Health, Substance Use and Recovery to consider all matters concerning behavioral health, mental illness and substance use disorder, including prevention and early intervention, treatment, recovery services, homelessness, and such other matters as may be referred, It is the Committee’s responsibility to oversee the Department of Mental Health and the Bureau of Substance Addiction Services.
