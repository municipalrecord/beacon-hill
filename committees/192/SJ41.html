Special Joint Committee on Redistricting

SJ41  ·  192nd General Court  ·  House chair: Michael J. Moran  ·  Senate chair: William N. Brownsberger

It shall be the duty of the Special Joint Committee on Redistricting to study a new division of the Commonwealth into (a) Congressional districts under the United States Constitution, (b) 40 senatorial and 8 councillor districts under Section 2 of Article CI of the Amendments to the Constitution, as amended, and (c) 160 representative districts under section 1 of said Article CI.
