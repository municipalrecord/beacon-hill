House Committee on Personnel and Administration

H45  ·  188th General Court  ·  House chair: William C. Galvin

It shall be the duty of the committee on Personnel and Administration to develop, implement and make future changes in a plan providing for the allocation of office space for members and staffs of the various committees under the jurisdiction of the House, to establish a plan containing staffing levels for committees under the jurisdiction of the House together with a classification plan for employees, and to facilitate the training of members and staff.
