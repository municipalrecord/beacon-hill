Seats

58 seats had more than one holder inside one court. Each has a page listing the holders in order and the gap between them.

Party is the ballot party, labelled as elected. A date at the end of a mid-court service is the Journal’s date for the departure, with its reason; where none is printed it is the last vote on file, a bound. Posts before 2017 beyond the presiding officer are not on file. The notes say all of this in full.
