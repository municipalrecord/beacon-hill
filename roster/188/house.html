Notes

  1. Party is the ballot party, “as elected”. An asterisk marks a row where the legislature’s feed lists a different party; see the note.
  2. Posts. For this court the feed names only the presiding officer; floor leaders, whips and the rest are not on file, which is a gap in our sources, not proof that no one held them. See the note.
  3. Dates served start on the day the court convened, or on the election date for a special-election winner. A member who left mid-court ends on the date the Journal prints for the departure, with the reason; where no Journal prints one, on the last vote on file, a bound. See the note.
  4. Namesakes. Three representatives in this court share a surname with another member (Walsh, Murphy, Hunt). The sheets print only the surname, and the site places those votes with a hand table. See the note.
