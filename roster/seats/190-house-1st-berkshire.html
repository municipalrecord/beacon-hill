The seat changed hands: Gailanne M. Cariddi to John Barrett, after John Barrett won the special election of 7 November 2017. Vacancy window on file: from Gailanne M. Cariddi’s last vote on file (14 June 2017) to John Barrett’s first vote on file (15 November 2017); the election fell on 7 November 2017. The Journal does not print the day of the oath. The running head of the sitting reads “Wednesday, November 15, 2017 [being the sitting of November 13]” (House Journal 2017, p. 967), which prints two days, so the day of the oath is not settled. Why the seat changed hands: Died on Saturday 17 Jun 2017 (House adjourned in memory); the Journal of Monday 19 Jun 2017 says 'last Saturday'; the date is worked out. Date checks: 3 passed, 0 failed, 3 could not run (a missing source is never counted as a pass). Passed: the election came on or before the arriving member's first vote on file; the departing member's last vote on file is before the election; the two members' votes on file never overlap. Could not run: the Journal's left date is on or after the departing member's last vote; the Journal's sworn-in date falls inside the window the rule allows; the Journal shows the departing member left before the arriving member was sworn in.
