One hole, the state's

In the 188th General Court, 180 votes carry no member code at all in the legislature's own data feed. One seat's votes in 2013 and 2014 are anonymous at the source. They are left out of every rate above rather than guessed at.

The legislature's roster for the 186th General Court carries no party for anyone, and nothing is carried back from a later court, because people change parties. So no party rate above uses a vote from 2009 or 2010, and a senator who served only then shows no party. Every other rate counts those years in full.

A senator appears here only with at least 100 roll calls, and a rate is shown only where it rests on at least 60 votes. Below that a percentage is noise rather than a comparison.
